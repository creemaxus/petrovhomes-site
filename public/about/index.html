About Max Petrov

A real estate agent who looks at homes the way a builder does.

Max Petrov is the real estate agent behind Petrov Homes, helping people buy and sell in Snohomish and King counties.

His approach comes from 10+ years of construction and flipping experience. That background shapes how he walks through a property: how it has been maintained, what a renovation would realistically involve, and which updates are likely to matter to the next owner.

For buyers, that means better-informed conversations about condition and potential. For sellers, it means practical guidance on what to fix, what to refresh, and what to leave alone.
