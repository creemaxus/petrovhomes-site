Meet Max Petrov

Max Petrov helps buyers and sellers across Snohomish and King counties. He brings 10+ years of construction and flipping experience to the way he evaluates homes: how a house was built, how it has been maintained, and what a change would actually involve. That helps buyers weigh condition and renovation potential, and it helps sellers decide which preparation is worth doing. Max keeps the conversation clear, works alongside your inspector and lender, and meets with clients in English or Russian.
