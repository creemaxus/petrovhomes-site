and none of the images below require visible attribution.

git-ignored and never deployed. The public copies were resized to 480, 800,
and 1024 px wide, re-encoded as AVIF and WebP, and stripped of all metadata
(EXIF, GPS, XMP, ICC).

No attribution is required. These are not listing photos, and a city is named
only when the photograph’s location was verified.

The Edmonds and Mukilteo files above are the only verified community photographs
in the project. Lynnwood, Everett, Bothell, and Mill Creek are linked by name
without a photograph, because no suitably licensed, location-verified image was
available. No interior or generic house exterior with a no-attribution license
was found, so service cards use these three scenes.
